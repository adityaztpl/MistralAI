# 12 - Captions and text

Captions, subtitles, and typography turn a good motion graphic into accessible, platform-ready video. This section covers caption ingestion, animated caption components, text layout, typography, and subtitle export formats.

## Learning goals

- Understand caption data shapes and timing in frames and seconds.
- Build animated captions that highlight words or phrases without losing readability.
- Lay out text predictably across square, landscape, and vertical compositions.
- Export SRT/VTT-style subtitle files alongside rendered video.
- Avoid typography pitfalls around safe areas, fonts, contrast, and localization.
